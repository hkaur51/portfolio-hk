The 9 datasets behind my corpus

I kept 9 public intraoral photograph datasets after quality control. Each link opens its original publication or repository.

  1. Dental CariesAhmed et al. 2025, Scientific Data · YOLO · caries, healthyOpen source
  2. OMNI (Malocclusion)Xue et al. 2025, arXiv · COCO JSON · malocclusion, orthodontic devices, normalOpen source
  3. Intelligent DiagnosticLiu et al. 2024, BMC Oral Health · PNG masks · calculus, gingivitis, cariesOpen source
  4. Oral CancerPiyarathne et al. 2024, Oral Oncology · COCO JSON · oral cancer, benign, OPMD, normalOpen source
  5. DentalAI (DatasetNinja)Valluri 2023, Kaggle · COCO JSON · cavity, caries, crack, healthyOpen source
  6. GingivitisDuy et al. 2024, Data in Brief · YOLO · 7-grade severity scaleOpen source
  7. Oral LesionsCheng et al. 2026, International Dental Journal · COCO JSON · benign ulcers, leukoplakia, lichen planusOpen source
  8. Ulcers (Autooral)Jiang et al. 2024, Scientific Reports · PNG masks · oral ulcersOpen source
  9. Kaggle Healthy TeethKaggle · YOLO · tooth types, mapped to healthyLink coming
